## LUCY

LUCY has three looks. Each one has a sheer veil (smoke or fabric) and a patterned paisley wall behind her.

| Look | Colours | Details |
|---|---|---|
| Night | Black, teal, gold | Gold face patterns with dots and bands, teal smoke veil, nose ring |
| Ember | Orange, blue | Silver-blue hair, smoke veil, warm orange light |
| Clay | Red clay, pale green | Sheer green veil, carved clay wall |
