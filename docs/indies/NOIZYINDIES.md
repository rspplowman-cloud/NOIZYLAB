# NOIZYINDIES: small Canadian indies meet, share, and become NOIZYFRIENDS

**For:** Robert Stephen Plowman (RSP_001). **Written:** 2026-11-11.
**Idea:** give small independent labels a place to meet, share costs, and keep 75/25 and ownership.
The opposite of a catalogue buyer: nobody sells their masters to join.

---

## 1. Why now

- **Federal top-up at risk.** $16 million a year that lets FACTOR and Musicaction invest in Canadian artists and
  Canadian-owned music companies was a temporary top-up, set to expire on **31 March 2026**. CIMA warned that,
  without renewal, that investment could be **cut by up to 50%**. The budget was tabled on 4 November 2026;
  **the outcome is not confirmed here** — check it.
- **The big buyers are buying.** Reservoir Media (NASDAQ: RSVR; has a Toronto office) spent about **$120 million** on
  acquisitions and advances in its last fiscal year, including the Nacional Records catalogue and master rights from
  Fool's Gold Records. Small labels under pressure are the next sellers.
- **The covenant math fits small labels.** `docs/economics/75-25-SURVEY-AND-MODEL.md`: lean spend + 75/25 clean
  works; reissues (no recording cost) fit it best.

## 2. What a NOIZYINDIES member gets

| Offer | What it means | Why it helps a small label |
|---|---|---|
| **Keep everything** | Masters and publishing stay with the label and artist. No buyout, ever. | The opposite of selling to a catalogue buyer. |
| **75/25 clean** | Artists get 75% after hard costs at invoice. NOIZY's tools, not NOIZY's cut. | A fair deal they can show their artists. |
| **Shared pressing pool** | Members combine vinyl runs at one plant. | Bigger runs lower the per-LP cost, the cost that breaks 75/25 on vinyl. |
| **Consent ledger** | Every recording and voice gets a fingerprint and consent record (`services/ledger`). | Proof against AI misuse; a selling point. |
| **Shared tools** | Distribution, metadata, royalty statements, LUCY/GABRIEL help. | Overhead near 10%, which is what makes 75/25 work. |
| **NOIZYFRIENDS circle** | A monthly online meet-up, and a yearly in-person one (Montreal first). | Indies stop working alone. |
| **Grant help** | One shared calendar and templates for FACTOR, Musicaction, Canada Council, provincial funds. | Small labels lose grants to paperwork. |
