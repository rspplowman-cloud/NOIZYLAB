## The invitation (draft, RSP_001's voice, to edit)

> Subject: An invitation from one Canadian indie to another
>
> Hello [name],
>
> I'm Robert Stephen Plowman, a composer and sound designer, and the founder of NOIZY.
>
> I'm inviting five independent Canadian labels to a small circle called NOIZYINDIES. No fee. No contract to sign.
>
> What it is:
> - You keep your masters and your publishing. Always.
> - A shared deal you can show your artists: 75% to the artist after hard costs.
> - A consent record for every recording and voice, so there is proof if AI misuses your work.
> - A monthly call where small labels share what works: pressing, grants, distribution.
>
> What I'm asking: thirty minutes on a call, to hear what is hardest for you right now.
>
> If it's not for you, no reply needed, and thank you for the records.
>
> Robert Stephen Plowman
> NOIZY · Fish Music Inc.

**Honest notes for RSP_001**
- The shared pressing pool, grant calendar and royalty tools are **plans**, not live. The letter only promises a call
  and the principles. Keep it that way until the tools exist.
- Check Canada's anti-spam law (CASL): a first business-to-business message is allowed when it is relevant to the
  recipient's role and their address is published without a "no unsolicited mail" note. Include your mailing
  address and a way to say "no more".
